Penstemon hybrida 'Pristine Lila Purple'

Wholesale perennial liners grown in Langley, BC

Penstemon hybrida 'Pristine Lila Purple' is propagated by Westcan Greenhouses as a wholesale plug/liner for growers, landscapers and re-wholesalers across Canada and the US. Hardy in zones 3–8. It is one of 21 Penstemon selections in the Westcan range.

Like everything we grow, it leaves our Langley, BC greenhouses as a uniform, well-rooted tray — on carts, boxed, or as palletized freight. First trays of the season are forecast for WK45 2026 — book ahead to lock in varieties and delivery weeks.
