Ground Cover Plugs, Wholesale

We grow 105 ground cover varieties as wholesale plugs — the fillers, spillers and mat-formers that landscape contracts and container programs burn through: Ajuga, Sedum, Thymus, Sagina (Irish and Scotch moss), Pachysandra, Lamium, Lysimachia, Delosperma and native Arctostaphylos uva-ursi among them.

Ground covers ship as dense, well-established plugs that knit in fast. For big landscape takeoffs, send us the plant schedule — we’ll quote the full list and gate deliveries by phase. For garden centre programs, mixed orders across varieties are no problem.
